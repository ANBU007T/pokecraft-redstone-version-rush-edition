========================================================
  ⚡ PokéCRAFT — Pokémon Redstone (Rush Edition v1.0.1)
========================================================

🎮 HOW TO PLAY:
1. Double-click "PokeCraft.exe" (or "Play-PokeCraft.bat").
2. The game starts instantly in offline desktop mode!
3. No installation, no internet, and no browser required.

--------------------------------------------------------
🕹️ CONTROLS & SHORTCUTS:
--------------------------------------------------------
  • Movement       : W / A / S / D  (or Arrow Keys)
  • Mine / Attack  : Left Click  (or Z)
  • Place / Eat    : Right Click (or X)
  • Jump           : Spacebar
  • Sprint         : Shift  (runs at 1.6x speed across Kanto)
  • Inventory      : E  (Minecraft inventory & crafting)
  • Drop Item      : Q
  • Hotbar         : 1 - 9  (or Mouse Wheel)
  • Pokémon Menu   : Enter (Party, Pokédex, Bag, Trainer Card)
  • Town Map       : M (or J)
  • Settings       : F1 (or Esc)
  • Remap Keys     : F2
  • Quick Save     : F5  (Saves across Slots 1-6 with screenshots)
  • Quick Load     : F8
  • Fullscreen     : F11

--------------------------------------------------------
🌐 MULTIPLAYER CO-OP (CABLE CLUB):
--------------------------------------------------------
  • Talk to the Cable Club receptionist in any Pokémon Center
    (or click "> JOIN CO-OP <" on the title screen).
  • TO HOST A WORLD:
    1. Select "HOST".
    2. Your game opens a 5-letter Room Code (e.g. KT4RP)
       and displays your LAN IP (e.g. 192.168.1.14:5901).
    3. Share the code/IP with your friends!
  • TO JOIN A WORLD:
    1. Select "JOIN".
    2. Enter the 5-letter Room Code.
    3. If playing across Wi-Fi or LAN, enter the Host's IP.
       (If playing on the same PC, leave Host IP blank).
    4. Choose your name & skin (Alex, Zuri, Kai, etc.) 
       and click JOIN!
    5. Explore Kanto together in real-time!
  • CHAT:
    Press T in-game to chat with friends.
